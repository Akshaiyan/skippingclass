# Product

## register
product

## Users
Claims handlers and team leads at a motor insurer, reviewing one claim at a time in an office, under time pressure. The approver signs off policy changes. Assumption, to confirm with the team.

## Product purpose
Shows a recommendation for each motor claim (routine processing, request information, or human review), the rule that decided it, the facts used, and what would change it. Records every reviewer correction and policy approval in a tamper-evident log.

## Brand and tone
Plain, exact and calm. The product says what the engine decided and why, and never implies approval or payment. No marketing voice.

## Anti-references
- Dark, neon, "AI dashboard" styling with gradients and glowing accents.
- Playful illustrations, mascots, or decorative icons.
- Hero metrics that show a big number with no explanation.

## Strategic principles
1. The reviewer decides; the engine explains and records.
2. Every colour carries a text label as well (never colour alone).
3. Records are append-only, so the memory shows what happened, not what we would like to show.
4. A demo can break; the screen must say so plainly, not hide it.
